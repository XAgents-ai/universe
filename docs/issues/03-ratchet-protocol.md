# Pre-register the physical-inheritance ratchet experiment R(W,B)

Define fresh-agent capability probes at fixed inference/action budget B indexed by predecessor resource/labor expenditure W. Compare intact history, physically disrupted organization with matched accessible usable energy, descriptions-only inheritance, and transplanted autonomous machines. Pre-register agent-source/knowledge conditions, physics family selections, null hypotheses, seeds, attrition and negative reporting.

Acceptance: executable experiment manifests and comparators, not claims about emergence; baseline no-agent/random/scripted entrants; quantify inherited working infrastructure separately from energy stockpiles and information transfer. Do not score merely on agent narrative.
